# Sources, dependencies and scope / 来源与依赖

## Dual-hand retrofit / 双手改装

Original frame designer: **Jiarong Lin / HKU MARS**, [Livox_handheld](https://github.com/hku-mars/Livox_handheld).

The measured interface was taken from commit [`31def48c50816932e5c5043d54c80a51fb80a6bf`](https://github.com/hku-mars/Livox_handheld/tree/31def48c50816932e5c5043d54c80a51fb80a6bf). The six custom adapter part types were generated separately in CadQuery; their previous delivery designated them GPL-2.0-only. Source and license notices are preserved here. The retrofit has not been endorsed or validated by the original authors.

Required upstream STLs are in the deliberately misspelled [`realease` directory](https://github.com/hku-mars/Livox_handheld/tree/31def48c50816932e5c5043d54c80a51fb80a6bf/realease):

- `all_parts_holder.STL` ×1
- `hand_hold_part_v3.STL` ×2
- `laptop_support_part_1_V5.STL` ×1
- `laptop_support_part_2.STL` ×3
- `laptop_support_part_3_v3.STL` ×3

对应原生SolidWorks源和上游装配说明请从同一固定版本获取。这些原件不在本库。上游附GPLv2许可证，并在README另列商业使用联系作者的说明；本库不替上游作额外许可承诺。

The camera reference used during the earlier dual-hand design was the [RealSense ROS D435 mesh](https://github.com/realsenseai/realsense-ros/blob/ros2-master/realsense2_description/meshes/d435.dae), under its own Apache-2.0 notices. That mesh and its combined Blender scenes are not distributed here. A D435 preview mesh is not a tolerance-controlled D435i manufacturing model.

## Single-hand v2 and laptop B1 / 单手与笔记本

Recovered custom design snapshots dated 2026-10-10 and 2026-10-11, respectively. Both CAD scripts construct solids from basic geometry without importing a vendor mesh. Their camera references are simple box envelopes. B1's laptop, fastener and marker illustrations are also custom simplified geometry, not downloaded product CAD or physical-test photographs.

B1's diagrams and manual were generated by `build_B.py`, `render_B.py`, `make_figures.py` and `make_manual.py`. The original manual's nominal dimensions and annotations must be updated when the model changes. Fonts are referenced by the scripts; no font binaries are bundled.

原包未明示v2/B1开源许可；发布者于2026-10-14确认本库自制文件统一采用GPL-2.0-only，范围见根目录 `LICENSING.md`。本次不以双手上游作者的名义发布这些独立设计，也不暗示厂家认可。

## Device dimensions / 设备尺寸依据

- [RealSense D400 Series Datasheet, March 2026](https://www.realsenseai.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/RealSense-D400-Series-Datasheet-Mar-2026.pdf): basis for dual-hand/B1 device constraints, including rear M3 interface. No copy of this datasheet is bundled.
- [Intel D400 Series Datasheet, May 2020](https://www.realsenseai.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Intel-RealSense-D400-Series-Datasheet-May-2020.pdf): source cited in the original single-hand v2 documentation (337029-008, Table 3-44 and Figure 10-8).

Measure real hardware and consult current manufacturer mounting requirements before use. No official laptop dimensional CAD was available for the B1 fit; its computer envelope remains illustrative.

## Excluded assets / 未收入

Downloaded Livox original CAD/STLs; manufacturer camera CAD/meshes; combined dual-hand Blender scenes; legacy dual-hand renders and PDF using those models; personal photographs; unrelated research/data; credentials; repository history from other projects.

For compact publication, B1 previews are JPEG copies of the original PNGs at unchanged pixel dimensions; complete manual text is included instead of the large image-rich PDF. All diagram/handbook source scripts are provided. No geometry was changed.
